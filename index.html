        How to install and share

        On iPhone: open this page in Safari, tap the Share button, then Add to Home Screen. Your readings stay saved on this phone as long as you keep using this same address.

        Each person who opens the app URL gets their own copy on their own phone. Use Share link to send a snapshot of your current readings. Opening a shared link never overwrites the other person’s saved data unless they choose Save to my tracker.

        If you opened a shared link, add the plain app address to your Home Screen — not the long shared URL.

        How to use the map

        Tap an item in the row above the map to add it. Drag items to move them. Drag an empty spot to pan. Pinch, or use + and −, to zoom.

        Tap an item to label it, link it to a tracker location, rotate it, or delete it. A linked item shows its phase pair and amps from the Tracker tab.

        Tap Cable run, pick a colour, then tap the two items it connects. Tap a cable to change its colour or add a length.

        The map starts on Lot: the Days Inn parking lot at 50 Brady St, Sudbury, drawn to scale from OpenStreetMap, so a 40 ft trailer is the right size. Grid is a blank grid. Satellite shows Esri aerial imagery of the same lot (needs data).

        Load lot photo puts a photo or screenshot of the lot behind the map. It is shrunk so it fits on your phone. Share links carry the layout. A photo is usually too big for a link, so it is left out when needed.
